Know the event before it happens.

Prop Firm News Alert is an economic-calendar awareness app for iPhone and iPad. It brings scheduled events, impact levels, local times, live countdowns and reminders into one focused place so you can prepare around your own prop-firm rules.

How it helps

Built for preparation, not guesswork

  • See upcoming scheduled events before their release time.
  • Reduce the chance of overlooking an important calendar event.
  • Understand whether an event is marked high, medium or low impact.
  • Use a live countdown instead of manually calculating the remaining time.
  • Keep event timing aligned with your preferred timezone.
  • Set local reminders for events you want to review later.
  • Focus on currencies relevant to your account or evaluation.
  • Prepare a personal checklist before reviewing the rules of your firm.

How to use it

From calendar to reminder in six steps

1

Choose a date range

Open Today, Tomorrow or Full Week from the calendar screen.

2

Apply filters

Select all, high, medium or low impact and choose your preferred currencies.

3

Check the time

Review the exact local date, event time and live countdown.

4

Open event details

Review the available actual, forecast and previous values for context.

5

Set an alert

Enable a local reminder if you want the app to notify you near the event.

6

Verify your rules

Before taking any action, check the current news-trading rules of your own prop firm.

Who it is for

Useful for anyone tracking scheduled market events

The app is designed for users following prop-firm evaluations, funded accounts, forex markets or economic releases who want a simple calendar and reminder tool. No brokerage account is required, and the app does not execute trades.

Important accuracy & compliance note

Prop Firm News Alert is an independent informational tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or an official product of any prop firm, broker or economic-calendar provider.

Event schedules, impact labels and values can be updated, delayed or revised. A displayed event window or status is only a user-configured awareness aid—it does not guarantee that trading is safe, permitted or compliant.

Every firm can apply different restrictions by account type, instrument, order type or event. Always verify the latest official rules of your own firm. The app does not provide financial advice, investment advice, trade signals or trade execution.
