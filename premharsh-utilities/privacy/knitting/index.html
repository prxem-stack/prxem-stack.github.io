Privacy policy

Effective 6 September 2026 · Android and iOS

This policy explains the information used by Bunai Counter, published by PremHarsh Utilities and maintained through the GitHub account prxem-stack.

Your records and advertising

Your utility records are saved in local app storage. Google’s advertising SDK communicates with advertising services, and exports leave the app when you choose a sharing destination.

1. Information you put in the app

This app can store the following information you enter or select:

  • Project names and notes.
  • Row or counter values, targets and repeat settings.

Projects and counter progress are saved on your device. The app does not publish your projects or connect to a pattern marketplace.

We do not operate an app-account service, developer database or cloud-sync service for this version. The app does not attach the content of these records to its advertising requests.

2. Advertising and privacy choices

The mobile app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), with banners and occasional full-screen ads at task breaks. Ad availability depends on the network, service eligibility and applicable privacy settings.

Google’s SDK collects or processes information such as your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device or app identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses advertising data for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. On iOS, access to an advertising identifier depends on the device’s tracking-permission settings.

The app requests non-personalized ads. This does not turn off all advertising data processing or remove consent requirements. Google’s User Messaging Platform checks whether a configured privacy message is required. When a privacy-options entry point is required, Settings displays “Manage ad privacy choices”. You can use it to revisit available choices.

Google’s processing is described in its Privacy Policy, partner-app information, and the SDK’s Android and iOS data disclosures. Google may process information on servers outside your country. Its policies explain its safeguards, retention and available controls.

3. Exports, sharing and device backups

Sharing or exporting project information can include the notes and pattern details you entered.

Sharing uses the system share sheet. The app or service you select receives the shared content and handles it under its own privacy policy. Copies saved to Files, photos, messages or other destinations are separate from this app’s local records.

Operating-system or device backups may copy app data according to your own backup settings. We do not control those backups and do not provide an in-app restoration service.

4. Storage, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall, subject to your device’s backup behavior. Use the app’s edit/delete controls for individual records or the confirmed “Delete all data” action in Settings for this app’s saved data. Important data should be exported first. The Settings JSON export is a copy for your records; this version does not import it back.

Deleting local app data does not delete copies already shared, operating-system backups, advertising information held by Google, or public GitHub posts. Manage those copies through the relevant destination or provider.

You can also review device advertising and tracking controls. No internet connection is needed for the core utility records; ads and some sharing destinations need connectivity. This app does not include a GPS, contacts-reading or microphone-recording feature.

Protect access to your device. We do not promise that device storage, exports or any online service is immune to loss or unauthorized access.

5. Support and privacy questions

Contact PremHarsh Utilities through the GitHub support forms. A GitHub account is needed to submit a request. Issues and comments are public. Send a general, non-sensitive description only. Do not post customer details, phone numbers, photos or signatures, financial records, passwords, advertising identifiers or personal documents.

Support posts expose the GitHub username and content you submit to us and the public; we use them to respond and improve the apps. Their hosting and account data are handled by GitHub under its Privacy Statement. This is not a private channel for identity documents or sensitive rights requests. See Support for local deletion and reporting guidance.

6. This website and policy changes

The app-information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add website advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts or a contact form that stores submissions here. GitHub may process visitor connection information to deliver and secure its services. Read the website privacy information.

We will update this policy when the app’s data handling changes. The effective date above identifies this version.
