Before you post

GitHub issues are public.

Everyone can read your report and any attachments. Use made-up examples. Leave out names, phone numbers, customer measurements, work or payment records, travel plans, images, signatures, passwords and device identifiers.

Do not upload your app’s data export. A general description is usually enough to begin.

Common questions

A few quick answers.

Where can I download the apps?

Android and iPhone store releases are in preparation. Each app’s page will show an official store link when available. There are no public download links on this website yet.

Where are my records saved?

Each app saves its own records in local app storage. This version has no app account or cloud sync. Your operating-system backup settings are separate. Advertising services process their own technical data; see the app’s privacy policy.

Can I restore a Settings JSON export?

The JSON export is a data copy for your records. These versions do not have an import or restore tool. Keep important shared reports and photos somewhere you can access before uninstalling or changing phones.

How do I delete my app data?

Use the app’s delete controls for individual entries, or open Settings and choose the confirmed Delete all data action. Export anything important first. This does not erase copies you shared, operating-system backups, advertising-provider data or GitHub support posts.

Why are ads sometimes missing?

Ads depend on internet connectivity, advertising-service availability, app review status and applicable privacy choices. The core utility remains usable when an ad is unavailable.

How can I change ad privacy choices?

When Google’s consent interface requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings shows Manage ad privacy choices. You can also review the advertising/tracking settings on your device. The privacy policies link to Google’s data information.

What helps when something goes wrong?

Give the app name, Android or iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce and what you expected. If an example needs data, use fictional values. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app before preserving important local records.

How do I raise a privacy question?

Use the general privacy-question form and keep it non-sensitive. Local utility records are controlled on your device, so their contents do not need to be sent to us. GitHub issues are public and are not a private channel for identity documents or sensitive personal requests. For information processed by Google or GitHub, use the controls and contact options in their linked privacy policies.
