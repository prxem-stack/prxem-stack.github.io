Good to know

Inputs: JPEG/PNG up to 12 MB and 20 MP. Outputs: JPEG, 1–2048 KB and dimensions up to 2400 pixels. Some image/size combinations cannot meet the requested limit. HEIC and animated images are not supported.

*The core utility works with local data. Ads require internet. A sharing destination may also need a connection.
