How do I play?

Collect the diamond key and reach the green exit before the 45-second timer ends. Complete six rooms. The thief steals a successful trick between rooms, so change your route and use the movement, Dash and Leap controls.

Where is my score?

Your best run stays on this device. There is no score account or cloud backup operated by the developer. Clearing app data or the browser game's site storage can remove local scores.

Why did an ad appear?

Mobile versions can show an available ad after a completed six-room run. Gameplay continues even if an ad is unavailable. The browser version does not use the mobile ad SDK.

How do I change ad choices?

Use “Ad privacy choices” in the mobile app when the consent system requires this control. Your device also offers advertising and privacy settings.

How do challenge links work?

The link opens the browser version with the same seed and stolen rules. Each player's score remains on their own device. It does not compare scores through an online leaderboard.
