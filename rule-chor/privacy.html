Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · Updated 17 October 2026 · Operator: Prem Harsh

Contact: premharsh.official@gmail.com

This policy covers the RULE CHOR browser game and Android/iOS mobile versions. The mobile versions include Google AdMob advertising; the browser game does not include the mobile advertising SDK.

Your game information

RULE CHOR calculates its adaptive rules on your device. Gameplay does not contact a language-model service. Best scores and escaped-room counts are stored locally. Current-run moves, rules and seeds are processed locally and are not sent to a developer-operated score server. There is no cloud score account or global leaderboard. Gameplay requires no account and does not ask for location, camera, microphone or contacts access.

Sharing challenges

Sharing is optional. A challenge link contains the room seed, stolen rules and target room count; the message can contain your score. You choose the app and recipient in the system share sheet. Their services handle anything you share under their own policies.

Mobile advertising

The mobile app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. An available full-screen ad can appear on the result screen after a completed six-room run. Ads need internet access. Consent status is checked at launch, and required consent forms appear before ads are requested. An “Ad privacy choices” control is offered when the consent system requires one.

Google's SDK can collect and share IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These support advertising, measurement, SDK operation and fraud prevention. The Android SDK encrypts these transmissions with TLS. Nonpersonalized advertising is requested, and publisher personalization and publisher first-party IDs are disabled. These settings do not mean that no information is collected.

Starting with iOS version 1.0.0 (build 2), when available, RULE CHOR requests Apple's tracking permission after a successful six-vault run, while Google's consent system permits ads, before enabling AdMob. Both Apple's tracking authorization and Google's consent eligibility are required for the app to initialize or load ads. Declining tracking leaves gameplay available. With authorization, Google's advertising SDK may use device identifiers for third-party advertising, measurement and tracking. Apple's permission is separate from Google's consent choices. The consent SDK can process consent-related requests before ad eligibility is established. Earlier iOS build 1 did not request IDFA permission.

In that iOS build, if tracking is no longer authorized or Google's consent eligibility no longer allows ads, the app stops initiating new ad requests and discards cached or pending ads. Ads can resume only when both allow them. This does not erase information already sent to providers or shut down an SDK that has already initialized; provider diagnostics may continue during that running session. Device privacy settings and Google's controls also apply.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services. Advertising-provider retention and privacy controls are described by Google; we cannot delete provider-held information by clearing your local game.

Browser game and this website

Website hosting can process request information, including IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect a site. The live browser game is hosted through ChatGPT Sites; see OpenAI's privacy policy. The browser game's Google Fonts requests can also send request information to Google.

This privacy and support website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement. These privacy and support pages use system fonts and contain no advertising SDK or developer-added analytics.

Choices and deletion

Use the mobile “Ad privacy choices” control when offered, along with your device's advertising/privacy settings. Local best scores remain until app/browser storage is cleared. On Android you can clear app data; on iOS delete the app and its stored data; in a browser clear the browser game's site storage. These actions do not remove information already received by advertising or hosting providers.

If you email support, your email address and the information in your message are used to respond and investigate the issue. Correspondence can remain in the support mailbox until it is deleted. Email the contact above to request deletion of support correspondence or ask a privacy question; provider-held information follows that provider's controls and obligations. Avoid sending passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal data.

Children and updates

The mobile game is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not ask players to provide an age or other personal profile in the game. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support message, contact us so the message can be reviewed and removed as appropriate. Store audience settings and advertising consent rules apply to the mobile versions. This policy's update date will change when privacy practices change.
