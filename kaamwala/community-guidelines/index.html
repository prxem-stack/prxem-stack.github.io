Community Guidelines

Updated: 19 September 2026

These rules apply to profiles, jobs, service listings, bids, messages, images, ratings and reviews.

Be truthful

  • Post real work or services with clear descriptions, location and fair expectations.
  • Do not impersonate, create fake demand, manipulate ratings or claim qualifications you do not have.
  • Reviews must reflect a genuine experience and must not be bought or traded.

Keep people safe

  • No threats, harassment, hate, discrimination, stalking, sexual exploitation or content involving minors.
  • No dangerous, illegal or regulated services, weapons, drugs, trafficking, fraud or instructions that facilitate harm.
  • Do not pressure anyone to pay before confirming identity, work scope and terms.

Respect privacy and rights

  • Do not publish someone else’s phone number, precise address, identity document, financial information or private conversation without permission.
  • Upload only images and text you own or are allowed to use. No copyright or trademark infringement.
  • Never upload passwords, recovery codes, bank credentials, Aadhaar or other government identity documents in public content.

Report and block

Use Report for objectionable content or behaviour and Block to stop interaction with a user. These are separate controls. Manage blocked accounts under Profile > Safety & blocked accounts. Reports may lead to content removal, account restrictions or suspension. Email kaamwala.online@gmail.com for urgent safety support.

Enforcement

We consider context, severity, history and risk. We may warn users, remove content, limit features, suspend or terminate accounts, and cooperate with lawful requests. Repeated or severe violations can result in permanent removal.
