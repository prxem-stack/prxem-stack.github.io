KaamWala Support

We help with sign-in, recovery, account deletion, reports, blocked accounts, ads, jobs, services, bids, chats and technical problems.

Email support

kaamwala.online@gmail.com

Include your phone model, app version, a short description and a screenshot if safe. Never send a password, recovery code, bank credential or government identity document.

Safety report

Use the in-app Report and Block controls first. For urgent safety concerns, email with the subject “KaamWala Safety Report”. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Account access

Use the recovery code created at registration to reset your password. If you no longer have it, contact support. We verify ownership before making account changes.

Account deletion

Delete from Profile > Delete account or follow the public deletion instructions.

Response and availability

Support is provided by email. We aim to review safety reports promptly, but response time depends on severity and available information.
