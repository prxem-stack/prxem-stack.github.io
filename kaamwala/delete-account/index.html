Delete your KaamWala account

Deletion is permanent. Your profile, credentials, uploads, jobs, bids, messages and reviews are removed from the active database. Another customer’s job may remain with your worker assignment removed. Ongoing assignments may be cancelled.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to KaamWala.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and confirm permanent deletion.

Delete on the web

Use the secure deletion form on the official service domain:

Open deletion form

If you cannot sign in

Email kaamwala.online@gmail.com from a contact address you control and include the phone number linked to the account. We will verify ownership. Never send your password, recovery code or identity document by email.

What may remain

Provider security or operational logs may remain temporarily under provider retention practices, and information may be retained where law requires it. Copies another user already saved cannot be recalled.
