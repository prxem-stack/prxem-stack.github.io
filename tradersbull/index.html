Phone guide

How to estimate a payout

  1. Open “Payout → Bank”Choose the payout tool from the home screen.
  2. Select your firm or modelUse the payout split that applies to your account.
  3. Enter your profitFor example, enter $2,000 before the profit split.
  4. Add chargesReview withdrawal or processing deductions, if applicable.
  5. Read the INR estimateUse the result as an estimate; your bank rate and taxes can vary.

  3. Use the calendarFilter by date and impact to plan the session.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator show the exact amount my bank will credit?

It provides an estimate based on your inputs. Final conversion rates, fees, taxes and bank deductions can differ.

Does News Alert guarantee that my prop-firm account will not breach?

No. It helps you prepare, but every trader must check the latest official firm rules and manage their own trades.

Are firms from outside India supported?

The apps are designed for Indian traders while covering prop firms and forex events from around the world.
