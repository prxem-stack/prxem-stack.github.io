Prop Firm Calculator for iOS

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 12, 2026

Prop Firm Calculator is a calculator and trade-journal utility. Your calculator inputs and journal entries stay on your device; the developer does not operate an account system or a server that receives those entries.

Information stored on your device

The app uses local device storage to remember settings, selected account details, calculator inputs, daily results, and trade-journal entries. This information is used to provide the app's features and is not uploaded to the developer. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

Advertising and information processed by Google

The app uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. Depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, and the type of ad served, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device or advertising identifiers, where permitted;
  • advertising data and interactions with ads;
  • app interactions, diagnostic information, crash logs, and performance data.

This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, analytics, diagnostics, and improving ad services. Processing by Google and its partners is governed by their own policies. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app uses Google's consent platform to ask whether you consent to personalized advertising and related data processing. You may accept, refuse, or manage available purposes and vendors. You can revisit or withdraw your choice using the Privacy choices button inside the app when that option is required for your region. Ads may still be shown after you refuse consent, but they will be limited according to the applicable choice and law.

Cookies and similar technologies

The app itself uses local storage for the on-device features described above. Google and participating advertising partners may use device identifiers, local storage, or similar technologies to deliver, limit, secure, and measure ads, subject to your consent where required.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your calculator inputs or trade-journal entries and does not receive them. Advertising-related information may be shared with or processed by Google and the advertising partners identified in the consent message for the purposes described above.

Data retention and security

On-device app data remains in the app's local storage until you change it or remove the app. Advertising providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertising service, please use the contact method below.

International processing

Google and its advertising partners may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their legal safeguards and privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Prop Firm Calculator, contact the developer through the Prxem Apps support page. For information held by Google, use the privacy controls and contact options provided in Google's Privacy Policy.
